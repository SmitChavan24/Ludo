Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data CoinLudo (ludo.billtrack.me) collects, why, and what choices you have. CoinLudo is operated by S N Chavan (BillTrack), Panvel, Maharashtra, India, who is responsible for your data. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

In short: we use your Google name, email and photo to run your account, keep a record of your games and coins, and never sell your data. Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay, not by us.

1. What we collect

  • Account details from Google: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, when you sign in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Game data: the matches and tournaments you play, results, moves, your coin balance and every coin transaction, bonuses claimed, and referral codes used.
  • Purchases: the coin packs you buy, the amount, the date and the payment status and ID. Payments are made on pay.billtrack.me through Razorpay. Razorpay collects your card, UPI or bank details and contact number under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Notifications: if you turn on match alerts, your browser's push subscription (a technical address for sending notifications to your device).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and server logs, used for security, preventing abuse and fixing problems.
  • Guest play: guests get a temporary ID for one practice session. No name or email is collected.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to sign you in.
  • To run matches and tournaments, keep your coin balance correct, and show leaderboards. Your name and photo are shown to other players in games, tournaments and leaderboards.
  • To process coin purchases, confirm payments, and handle refunds and support requests.
  • To send match and tournament notifications, if you turn them on.
  • To detect cheating, fraud and abuse, and to keep CoinLudo secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your data because you consented when you signed in, to provide the service you asked for, and where the law requires it.

3. Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • Google, to sign you in.
  • Browser push services (such as Google, Mozilla or Apple), only to deliver notifications you turned on.
  • Our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services), which stores the data for us on servers in Australia. By using CoinLudo you agree to your data being stored there.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

4. Storage on your device

CoinLudo stores small items in your browser's local storage: your sign-in session, a referral code if you opened an invite link, and your sound setting. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: while your account is open.
  • Purchase and payment records: 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data, except records we must keep by law.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to our servers and database is restricted, and sign-in sessions expire and are rotated. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete your account and personal data;
  • withdraw your consent (this means closing your account).

Email us from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days. You can turn off notifications at any time in your browser settings. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

CoinLudo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
